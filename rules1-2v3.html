Rules 1-2

Rule 1

Visibility of System Status

The design should always keep users informed about what is going on and let people know what's happening, through appropriate feedback within a reasonable amount of time.


For example, a website should show a loading bar when a page is loading.

Rule 2

Match Between System and the Real World

The design should speak the users' language. Use words, phrases, and concepts familiar to the user, rather than internal jargon. Follow real-world conventions, making information appear in a natural and logical order.


Also, the design should feel natural and mimic how things work in the real world.
