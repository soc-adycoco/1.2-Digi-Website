Rules 3-4

Rule 3

User Control and Freedom

Users can often perform actions by mistake. They need a clearly marked "emergency exit" to leave the unwanted action without having to go through an extended process.


Give users control: Users should be able to easily undo actions and have control over what they're doing.

Rule 4

Consistency and Standards

Users should not have to wonder whether different words, situations, or actions mean the same thing. The design should follow platform and industry conventions (external consistency).


The same words, symbols, and actions should be used for the same things throughout the design so users don't get confused (internal consistency).
